EZCAD2 Pen Settings for slotted_disk_3p5.dxf
=============================================
ONE slotted graphite disk, finished OD = 3.5 mm, inner hole
radius = 0.55 mm, 16 symmetric radial slots extending to
the outer edge (no eddy-current ring remains).

All cuts use a 6-line / 125 um kerf pattern (was 4-line / 75 um).
The slot inner radius is shifted outward from 0.75 to
0.877 mm so the material bridge between adjacent slots at
the slot inner end is the same as the 4-line baseline (about
220 um arc length). Inner hole and outer perimeter
finished dimensions are unchanged.

Same through-cut recipe as before, but 6 cuts now:
  6 cuts at 25 um spacing (125 um kerf), 20 processes
  (Loop Count), 2000 mm/s, 75 % power, Mark Count ~ 20-30 cycles.

Kerf correction is applied so the finished disk edges land exactly at
the nominal radii (assuming a 20 um kerf).

Two pens / layers in the DXF:
  Blue   (ACI 5)  INSIDE   16 slots + inner cutout
  Black  (ACI 7)  OUTSIDE  outer perimeter
